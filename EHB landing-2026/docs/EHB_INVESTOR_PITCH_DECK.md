# EHB Investor Pitch – Outline

This file captures the slide content for an investor-ready pitch deck. It can be converted directly into
PowerPoint, Keynote, or PDF.

## Slide 1 – Title

**EHB Technologies**  
*The Future of Global AI-Powered Ecosystems*

---

## Slide 2 – Problem

Users and businesses today need multiple platforms:

- **Fiverr** – services and freelancers  
- **Amazon** – products and commerce  
- **LinkedIn** – jobs and professional identity  

This is:

- fragmented and hard to manage  
- repetitive (multiple profiles, reviews, wallets)  
- difficult to scale globally across industries  

---

## Slide 3 – Solution

**EHB combines all of these into ONE system.**

- Unified profile and verification  
- AI-powered matching and recommendations  
- Multi-industry marketplace and franchise engine  

One place to:

- find jobs  
- offer services  
- sell products  
- run franchises  

---

## Slide 4 – Product

- 32 industries (education, health, IT, logistics, retail, and more)  
- AI-powered matching and recommendations  
- Global marketplace for services, jobs, and products  
- Country / corporate / master / sub franchise model  

Screenshots should show:

- Landing page hero  
- AI insights dashboard  
- Marketplace view  
- Franchise dashboard mock

## Slide 5 – Business Model

Revenue streams:

- **Service fees** – small percentage on each successful order  
- **Franchise income** – upfront fees + revenue share by territory  
- **Premium subscriptions** – visibility boosts, lower fees, advanced analytics  
- **Token / blockchain ecosystem (future)** – EHBGC for lower fees, rewards, and premium access
