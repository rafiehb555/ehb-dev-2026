```
Legal services affect everyone, yet the industry remains stuck in the past. 
Finding a lawyer is confusing, expensive, and opaque. We're changing that
```

```
"Today, if you need legal help:
- You don't know which lawyer to choose

We solve all of these problems."
```

## BACKUP PLAN

If any feature doesn't work during demo:

1. **Voice not working?**
   - Switch to text input immediately
   - Say: "Let me type this for a clearer demonstration"

2. **Page loading slow?**
   - Have screenshots ready
   - Say: "While this loads, let me explain..."

3. **Animation glitches?**
   - Refresh page
   - Continue with explanation

4. **Investor asks about backend?**
   - Say: "We have a robust architecture designed for scale. 
     I'd be happy to arrange a technical session with our team."
