## How STL is Calculated

STL = combination of **4 sources**:

1. **PSS (Identity Trust)** — 0–40 points
   - ID verified? +5
   - Liveness passed? +5
   - Address verified? +5
   - AML clean? +5
   - Phone verified? +5
   - No fraud flags? +15
   - **Max: 40 points → L5 ADVANCED**

2. **CRB (Certification)** — exams, licenses, renewals
   - Exam pass? +20 points
   - Annual renewal done? +10 points
   - **Max: L9 ELITE**

3. **Franchise** — geographic trust, sub-franchises
   - City franchise approved? +25 points
   - State franchise? +35 points
   - Country franchise? +45 points
   - **Max: L8 VIP**

4. **DMO (Governance)** — manual review, activity
   - Active 30+ days? +10 points
   - 0 complaints? +15 points
   - DMO approval? +20 points
   - Founder approval? = **L10 SUPREME**

---

## The MIN-Chain Rule (Critical!)

```
YOUR FINAL STL = MINIMUM OF:
  • Your PSS score
  • Your CRB status
  • Your Franchise tier
  • Your Company's STL
  • Your Owner's STL
```

**Example:**
- You = L8 VIP (franchise owner)
- Your company = L3 NORMAL (not verified yet)
- **Your FINAL STL = L3** (the lowest!)

Even if **you're perfect**, if your **company is new**, you **both stay low** together. This prevents fraud.

## How to Level Up (5 Steps)

### 1. Complete PSS Verification (→ L5 MAX)

"Aap L3 se L5 tak jaa sakte ho sirf PSS se — 20 min mein."

**What to do:**
- [ ] Verify ID (CNIC/Passport) — photo + OCR
- [ ] Liveness test — blink, smile, head turn (anti-spoofing)
- [ ] Phone verification — SMS OTP
- [ ] Address proof — utility bill or bank statement
- [ ] AML check — automatic (if income >50K/month)

**Benefit:** Unlock 40% of your earning potential, lower fees, better search rank.

### 2. Build Activity (L4 → L5)

Complete **10+ good transactions** without complaints:
- [ ] 10+ sales/orders (GoSellr)
- [ ] 10+ successful services (WMS, OLS, etc.)
- [ ] Average rating ≥ 4.5 stars
- [ ] 0 chargebacks or disputes

**Benefit:** Automatic STL boost, system rewards activity.

### 3. Pass a CRB Exam (→ L6+)

"CRB exam pass karne se aap professional ban jaate ho."

**Professional exams available:**
- E-commerce seller certification (GoSellr)
- Lawyer certification (OLS — bar exam)
- Doctor certification (WMS — medical license)
- Consultant certification (HPS)
- Instructor certification (OBS — teaching credential)

**To qualify:**
- [ ] L5+ STL (must have good PSS first)
- [ ] 50+ transactions in your domain
- [ ] Pay exam fee (100–500 EHBGC)
- [ ] Pass 70%+ score

**Benefit:** Jump to L6+ professional tier, 10x visibility boost, premium pricing.

### 4. Apply for Franchise (→ L8)

"Aapka apna territory chalao aur L8 VIP ban jao!"

**Requirements:**
- [ ] L6+ STL
- [ ] KYB verification (business registration)
- [ ] Capital lock (Online=0, City=10K, State=50K, Country=100K EHBGC)
- [ ] DMO interview + approval

**Territory options:**
- Online (1 user) = FREE
- City (500K pop) = 10K EHBGC lock
- State (10M pop) = 50K EHBGC lock
- Country = 100K EHBGC lock

**Benefit:** Earn 15–25% on all sub-franchises, L8 VIP status, premium features.

### 5. Get DMO Nomination (→ L9/L10)

"L9 ELITE aur L10 SUPREME sirf DMO aur founder approve kar sakte hain."

**L9 ELITE (by DMO invitation):**
- 2+ years perfect record (0 complaints)
- 1000+ transactions
- Franchise L8 VIP
- DMO nominates you

**L10 SUPREME (by founder):**
- Perfect record (0 all-time complaints)
- 100K+ EHBGC locked
- Founder manual sign-off
- Rarest level — only 100s globally

## Common Mistakes (Avoid These!)

❌ **Don't:** Ignore PSS verification
- "I'll do it later" → You stay L3 forever
- **Do:** Complete PSS in day 1 → unlock L5 potential

❌ **Don't:** Rush exams without prep
- "I'll pass the CRB exam today" → Fail → wait 30 days to retry
- **Do:** Study first, pass 90%+, jump straight to L6

❌ **Don't:** Get complaints
- One complaint → STL drops 5–10 points
- **Do:** 100% customer satisfaction → STL climbs 2–3 points per sale

❌ **Don't:** Lock too little capital
- City franchise = 10K lock, but you only locked 5K → L5 max, not L8
- **Do:** Meet full tier requirement → unlock full STL
