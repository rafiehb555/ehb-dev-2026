# EHB Franchise Applicant Guide

## When to Use

- **English:** "I want to apply for an EHB franchise. What are the requirements?"
- **Roman Urdu:** "Mujhe EHB franchise chaiyhe. Mujhe kya karna padega?"
- **Trigger:** User interested in franchise, wants to know tiers, asking eligibility

---

## The 4 Franchise Tiers

Think of EHB franchises like **geographic territories**. Higher tier = bigger area = higher responsibility + higher earnings.

| Tier | Territory | Population | Capital Lock | Revenue Share | Max Users | STL Min |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| **Online** | Digital only | All regions | 0 EHBGC | 15% of sub-earnings | 1 (yourself) | L6 HIGH |
| **City** | Metropolitan area | 500K | 10K EHBGC | 20% + platform cut | Up to 100 | L6 HIGH |
| **State** | Provincial/state | 10M | 50K EHBGC | 25% + platform cut | Up to 1000 | L7 PRO |
| **Country** | National | 100M+ | 100K EHBGC | 40% + Founder seat | Unlimited | L8 VIP |

## Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

**Are you eligible? Answer these 5 questions:**

### Q1: What is your current STL?

```
L1–L5?  → ❌ Not eligible. Need L6+ minimum.
           Go do PSS + CRB exam first (2–3 months).

L6–L7?  → ✅ Can apply for Online or City.

L8+?    → ✅ Can apply for any tier (City/State/Country).
```

**How to get L6:** Complete PSS verification (20 min) + pass CRB exam (2–3 weeks study).

### Q2: Do you have a business registered?

```
No formal business?     → ❌ For City/State/Country, need KYB.
                            ✅ Online tier = fine (solo operator)

Yes, registered (SECP)? → ✅ Eligible. Provide registration doc.

Partnership or LLC?     → ✅ Eligible. All partners must verify ID.
```

**KYB (Know Your Business) required for City+:**
- Company registration (SECP, form A/B)
- Director ID + address
- Articles of Association (if applicable)

### Q3: Do you have the capital lock?

```
Online?  → 0 EHBGC needed (FREE)
City?    → Need 10K EHBGC. Do you have?
State?   → Need 50K EHBGC. Do you have?
Country? → Need 100K EHBGC. Do you have?
```

**Capital lock** = coins you must keep "frozen" in your wallet. You earn interest (2–5% annually), but can't withdraw for lock period.

- Online = 0 (no lock)
- City = 10K locked for 12 months
- State = 50K locked for 24 months
- Country = 100K locked for 36 months

### Q4: Can you run day-to-day operations?

For **City+**, you need **team:**

```
Online tier?   → Just you. Part-time OK.

City tier?     → Need 2–3 people:
                  1. You (franchise owner)
                  2. Operations manager
                  3. Support agent
                  Time: 20+ hours/week

State tier?    → Need 5–10 people
                  + sub-franchise managers
                  Time: Full-time CEO role

Country tier?  → Need 20+ staff
                  + regional managers
                  Time: Full-time CEO + board
```

### Q5: Have you read the Franchise Agreement?

```
Yes, understood → ✅ Ready to apply

No              → ❌ Must read first (15 min read)
                    Contains: revenue split, dispute rules,
                    data ownership, non-compete, exit terms
```

## Step 2: Choose Your Tier

```
"Mujhe kaun sa tier choose karna chahiye?"

IF you're just starting  → Online (FREE, no commitment)
IF you want to grow      → City (10K EHBGC, medium commitment)
IF you want to scale     → State (50K, major commitment)
IF you want premium      → Country (100K, exclusive)
```

**Recommendation flow:**

```
First-time?
  YES → Start with Online → Prove capability (3–6 months)
        → Then upgrade to City

Experienced (2+ years)?
  YES → Can jump to City or State directly

Big team + capital?
  YES → Go straight to State or Country
```

## Step 3: Gather Required Documents

### For ALL tiers:

- [ ] **Your ID (CNIC/Passport)** — photocopy or scan
- [ ] **Address proof** — utility bill (3 months current)
- [ ] **Bank statement** — last 3 months (to verify capital)
- [ ] **PSS verification** — complete before applying
- [ ] **No complaints history** — must have 0 active disputes

### For City tier (add to above):

- [ ] **Business registration** (SECP form, NTN certificate)
- [ ] **Director ID** (if not you)
- [ ] **Articles of Association**
- [ ] **Bank account** (in business name)
- [ ] **Proof of 10K EHBGC** (screenshot of wallet lock)

### For State tier (add to above):

- [ ] **Tax returns** (last 2 years)
- [ ] **Business license** (provincial level)
- [ ] **Office lease/ownership** (proof of physical space)
- [ ] **Team org chart** (names + roles)
- [ ] **Proof of 50K EHBGC lock**

### For Country tier (add to above):

- [ ] **Financial audit** (CPA-certified)
- [ ] **Board resolution** (if board exists)
- [ ] **National business registration**
- [ ] **Proof of 100K EHBGC lock**
- [ ] **Letter of intent** (why you want this tier)

## Step 4: Submit Application

**Where to apply:** `/pss/franchise` page (or ask support)

**What happens:**

```
1. Submit docs + KYB
   ↓
2. Auto-check (24h):
   - STL ≥ required? ✅
   - KYB clean? ✅
   - No complaints? ✅
   - Capital locked? ✅
   ↓
3. DMO review (3–7 days):
   - Franchise manager interviews you
   - Asks about team, plans, capacity
   - Video call (30 min)
   ↓
4. Decision (1–2 days):
   - Approved → You get franchise access
   - Rejected → Get feedback + can retry
   ↓
5. Contract signing (24h):
   - Sign digital agreement (on-chain)
   - Coin lock activated
   - You're live! 🎉
```

## Step 5: Understand Your Revenue Share

### Example: City Franchise

You run a City franchise earning **100K EHBGC/month** (aggregate of all sellers).

```
Total earnings      = 100K EHBGC
↓
EHB platform cut    = 40K (40%)
↓
Remaining to you    = 60K
↓
You keep            = 20K (20% of gross)
Your sub-franchises = 40K
```

**If you have 5 sub-franchises (Online tier):**
They each earn different amounts. You take 15% from their earnings.

## Common Mistakes (Avoid!)

### ❌ Mistake 1: Applying with low STL

"Mera STL L4 hai, apply kar du?"

→ **No.** You **must have L6+**. Spend 2 weeks doing:
1. Complete PSS verification (→ L5)
2. Do 10+ good transactions (→ L4–L5)
3. Study + pass CRB exam (→ L6)

### ❌ Mistake 2: Not having capital ready

"Mujhe 10K EHBGC chahiye lekin mere paas sirf 5K hai."

→ **No.** You must **lock exactly the required amount**. Options:
- Earn 5K more (takes 1–2 months)
- Borrow from franchise owner (they hold your lock, not ideal)
- Apply for smaller tier (Online = 0 lock)

### ❌ Mistake 3: Skipping PSS verification

"Mujhe CRB exam pass ho gaya, ab franchise apply kar du?"

→ **No.** CRB alone is **not enough**. You **also need**:
- PSS identity verification (L5 minimum)
- Active selling history (10+ transactions)
- 0 complaints

### ❌ Mistake 4: Choosing wrong tier

"Mujhe City deey do, lekin mera team sirf 1 aadmi hai."

→ **No.** City needs 2–3 people. Options:
- Hire 2 more staff first
- Apply for Online tier (just you)
- Wait 6 months, build team, then apply City

## After You're Approved

Once you get franchise approval:

1. **Your wallet** = automatically locked
   - "10K EHBGC locked for 12 months" (City example)
   - You earn 2–5% interest on locked amount
   - Can't withdraw, but can use for business

2. **Your STL** = locked at approval level
   - If approved as City, your STL = L8+ automatically
   - If your personal STL drops (complaint), franchise drops too (MIN rule)

3. **Your sub-franchises**
   - You can now approve Online franchises under you
   - Revenue from each sub = 15% to you

4. **Your dashboard**
   - `/franchise/dashboard` shows earnings, team, sub-franchises
   - Weekly payouts to your bank
