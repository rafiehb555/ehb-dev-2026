## EHB-STL Level Up Conditions

### L1 → L2 (FREE → BASIC)
- ✅ Email + Phone verified
- ✅ Profile complete

### L2 → L3 (BASIC → NORMAL)
- ✅ CNIC + Face Match (PSS complete)
- ✅ 3+ successful transactions

### L3 → L4 (NORMAL → STANDARD)
- ✅ Business / Service registered
- ✅ 5+ successful orders
- ✅ 4+ rating average

### L4 → L5 (STANDARD → ADVANCED)
- ✅ Documents fully verified
- ✅ 10+ successful orders
- ✅ Complaint rate < 10%

### L5 → L6 (ADVANCED → HIGH)
- ✅ CRB (Physical verification)
- ✅ 20+ successful orders
- ✅ Complaint rate < 5%
- ✅ Response time good

### L6 → L7 (HIGH → VIP)
- ✅ 50+ successful orders
- ✅ Complaint rate < 2%
- ✅ No fraud history
- ✅ Franchise / Premium verification
- ✅ 3 months consistency

### L7 → L8 (VIP → SUPREME 👑)
- ✅ DMO manual approval required
- ✅ 100+ successful orders
- ✅ Complaint rate < 1%
- ✅ Full blockchain verification
- ✅ Franchise authority verified
- ✅ 6 months VIP consistency
