- Company governance (day-to-day operations)
- Community governance (major decisions)
- Token-based voting (ecosystem rules)

# GOVERNANCE BODIES

## 1. EHB Foundation

**Role:** Legal custodian of ecosystem

**Responsibilities:**
- Intellectual property
- Legal compliance
- Treasury management
- Emergency decisions

---

## 2. Community Council

**Members:** Token holders, top contributors

**Responsibilities:**
- Feature proposals
- Ecosystem rules
- Reward adjustments
- Community initiatives

---

## 3. Franchise Council

**Members:** Corporate, Master, Sub franchises

**Responsibilities:**
- Regional policies
- Service quality standards
- Franchise expansion
- Local compliance

---

## 4. Core Team Council

**Members:** EHB leadership, key employees

**Responsibilities:**
- Technical decisions
- Product roadmap
- Security matters
- Operational efficiency

# VOTING MECHANISM

## Voting Power Calculation

Voting power based on multiple factors:

| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Token Holdings | 40% | Staked tokens |
| Contribution Score | 30% | Platform activity |
| Reputation (STL) | 20% | Trust level |
| Account Age | 10% | Time on platform |

**Formula:**
```
Voting Power = (Tokens × 0.4) + (Contribution × 0.3) + (STL × 0.2) + (Age × 0.1)
```

## Quorum Requirements

| Proposal Type | Quorum | Majority |
|---------------|--------|----------|
| Feature Update | 5% | 51% |
| Fee Change | 10% | 60% |
| Treasury Allocation | 15% | 66% |
| Major Protocol Change | 25% | 75% |
| Emergency Decision | 5% | 80% |

# GOVERNANCE AREAS

## What DAO Decides

| Area | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Ecosystem Upgrades | New features, integrations |
| Reward Allocation | Referral rates, staking rewards |
| Franchise Approvals | New region, partner approval |
| Fee Structures | Platform fees, verification costs |
| Community Programs | Events, grants, initiatives |

---

## What DAO Does NOT Decide

| Area | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Security Patches | Emergency action needed |
| Legal Compliance | Regulatory requirement |
| Individual Disputes | Handled by DMO |
| Technical Architecture | Core team expertise |
