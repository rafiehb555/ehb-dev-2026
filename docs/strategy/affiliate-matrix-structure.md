 # EHB AFFILIATE MATRIX & RANK MODEL

*(5×10 Matrix + 10-Rank Leadership + Industry Integration)*

This document extends the core EHB affiliate program into a **structured 5×10 matrix model** with a **10-rank leadership system** and **32-industry integration**, designed to stay simple yet scalable.

---

- **Width:** 5 (maximum 5 direct referrals per affiliate).
- **Depth:** 10 levels.

| Level    | Members   |
