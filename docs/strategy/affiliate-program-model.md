 # EHB AFFILIATE PROGRAM MODEL

 *(Referral & Growth Ecosystem)*

This document defines a **practical affiliate program** for EHB that supports:

- User and customer growth
- Provider and business onboarding
- Franchise and industry expansion

Affiliates earn when they **bring verified, revenue-generating activity** into the ecosystem.

---

## 1️⃣ AFFILIATE TYPES

High-level affiliate categories:

- **User Referral Affiliates**
  - Bring new customers who book services or buy products.
- **Provider/Business Affiliates**
  - Bring new service providers, professionals, or businesses onto EHB.
- **Franchise/Industry Affiliates**
  - Help recruit new franchises or industry partners.

---

## 2️⃣ CORE PROGRAM STRUCTURE

```text
EHB AFFILIATE ECOSYSTEM
│
├ User Referral Program
├ Provider Onboarding Program
└ Franchise & Partner Referral Program
```

Each stream has:

- Clear **qualification rules**
- **Tracked referrals** (via links/codes)
- Transparent **commission logic**

---

## 3️⃣ USER REFERRAL PROGRAM

**Goal:** Grow active customers.

Mechanics:

- Affiliates share **referral links or codes**.
- When referred users:
  - Complete **verified registrations**.
  - Make **successful bookings or purchases**.
- Affiliates earn:
  - One-time **signup bonuses** (optional).
  - **Percentage of platform commission** from referred user activity for a limited period.

Example:

- EHB takes 20% commission on a service.
- User-referral affiliate earns **10–20% of that commission** for the first 6–12 months.

---

## 4️⃣ PROVIDER / BUSINESS AFFILIATE PROGRAM

**Goal:** Onboard high-quality providers and businesses.

Mechanics:

- Affiliates refer:
  - Service providers.
  - Professionals.
  - Businesses and product sellers.
- When referred entities:
  - Complete **verification** (PSS + franchises + basic checks).
  - Reach **defined revenue milestones**.
- Affiliates earn:
  - Fixed **onboarding bonuses** per verified provider.
  - A small **share of platform commission** from that provider for an initial period.

This encourages:

- Quality-focused onboarding.
- Long-term provider success.

---

## 5️⃣ FRANCHISE & PARTNER AFFILIATE PROGRAM

**Goal:** Expand the **franchise network** and **industry partnerships**.

Mechanics:

- Affiliates introduce:
  - New **Corporate/Master/Sub Franchises**.
  - Key **industry authorities** or certification partners.
- When a partnership closes:
  - Affiliate can receive **one-time referral fee**.
  - Optionally, a **small share** of franchise revenue for a limited time.

This program is more selective and usually managed via:

- Direct agreements.
- Strategic partner onboarding.

---

## 6️⃣ TRACKING & ATTRIBUTION

Tracking methods:

- **Unique referral links**
  - For web and app signups.
- **Affiliate codes**
  - Applied during registration or onboarding.
- **Logged referrals** in DMO/EHB back office.

Data captured:

- Referrer ID.
- Referred user/business/franchise ID.
- Date/time and channel.
- Activity and revenue generated.

---

## 7️⃣ COMMISSION & PAYOUT MODEL

General rules:

- Minimum payout thresholds (e.g., **$50 equivalent**).
- Monthly or bi-weekly payout cycles.
- Payout via:
  - **EHB Wallet**.
  - Connected bank or payment providers.

Commission caps:

- Caps per referred entity.
- Caps per period to manage risk and sustainability.

---

## 8️⃣ COMPLIANCE & QUALITY GUARDRAILS

To protect the ecosystem:

- Prohibit:
  - Fake accounts.
  - Self-referrals and abuse.
  - Misleading marketing.
- AI + fraud systems:
  - Monitor unusual referral patterns.
  - Flag suspicious affiliates for review.
- Violations:
  - Commission reversals.
  - Affiliate program suspension or permanent ban.

Affiliates with high-quality referrals can be promoted to:

- **“Verified Affiliate” or “Partner” tiers** with better terms.

---

## 9️⃣ AFFILIATE DASHBOARD

Features:

- Real-time **statistics**:
  - Clicks, signups, verifications.
  - Bookings/orders from referred users.
  - Commissions and payouts.
- Links and tools:
  - Referral links and codes.
  - Marketing resource library.
- Compliance:
  - Program rules and guidelines.

---

## 🔟 INTEGRATION WITH TRUST & STL

Affiliate program aligns with trust infrastructure:

- Only **verified** providers and businesses count for full commissions.
- Affiliates rewarded more for referring:
  - High-STL, well-reviewed entities.
  - Low-complaint, high-quality activity.

This keeps growth **aligned with quality and trust**, not just volume.
