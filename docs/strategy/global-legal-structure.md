# EHB GLOBAL LEGAL STRUCTURE

> Multi-layer legal framework to operate EHB in 200+ countries

---

## 1. GLOBAL HOLDING COMPANY

Top-level legal entity:

```text
EHB GLOBAL HOLDING
        │
        ▼
Regional Companies
        │
        ▼
Country Companies
        │
        ▼
Franchise Network
```

Global holding responsibilities:

- Owns **intellectual property** (brand, software, patents).
- Operates and licenses the **technology platform**.
- Defines **global policies** and compliance frameworks.
- Manages **international partnerships** (banks, cloud, enterprise partners).

This entity is usually set in a jurisdiction suitable for:

- IP protection
- Tax optimization (while remaining compliant)
- Investment & funding

---

## 2. REGIONAL COMPANIES

World is divided into **regions**, each with a regional entity:

Example regions:

- Asia Region
- Europe Region
- Middle East Region
- Africa Region
- Americas Region

Regional companies handle:

- Region-specific regulations & standards.
- Language and localization requirements.
- Regional strategic partnerships (telcos, regulators, big enterprises).
- Coordination of **country-level subsidiaries** and franchises.

They act as a bridge between **Global Holding** and **Country Companies**.

---

## 3. COUNTRY OPERATING COMPANIES

Each target market has a **local operating company**.

Examples:

- `EHB Technologies Pakistan (Pvt) Ltd`
- `EHB Technologies UAE LLC`
- `EHB Technologies UK Ltd`

Country company responsibilities:

- Local **business registration** and licensing.
- **Tax registration** and reporting.
- Local employment and HR compliance.
- Contracts with local partners (banks, logistics, regulators).
- Operating and supervising **Corporate / Master / Sub franchises** in that country.

Benefits:

- Meets **local legal requirements**.
- Simplifies **contracts** and **dispute resolution** for local users and partners.

---

## 4. FRANCHISE LEGAL STRUCTURE

Franchise agreements define **legal rights and responsibilities**.

Franchise levels:

- **Sub Franchise** – city / district
- **Master Franchise** – region / province / state
- **Corporate Franchise** – country level

Key legal points in franchise contracts:

- **Inspection authority** (what they can verify, how).
+- **Verification responsibilities** (scope & standards to follow).
- **Revenue sharing** (exact % splits, payment terms).
- **Territory rights** (exclusive / non-exclusive areas).
- **Brand usage & quality standards**.
- **Termination & breach clauses** (in case of fraud/misconduct).

All franchise operations must **mirror DMO & CRB rules**, ensuring legal continuity from legal contracts → operational behavior.

---

## 5. DATA PRIVACY & DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Every country / region has different **data protection laws**:

- Europe → **GDPR**
- USA → **CCPA** and others
- Asia → local data protection & PECA-type laws

EHB must implement:

- **Regional data centers / clusters** (e.g. EU region, MENA region, APAC region).
+- Data residency rules: **user data stays within allowed geography**.
+- Strong data protection policies:
  - Encryption (at rest & in transit)
  - Access control & logging
  - Data minimization & retention policies

Relation to architecture:

- Connects with `multi-country-infrastructure.md` and `complete-data-architecture.md`.

Result:

- Legal compliance
- Better performance (regional latency)

## 6. GLOBAL CERTIFICATION AUTHORITY (CRB)

EHB’s **CRB (Certification & Registry Board)** can act as a **global certification authority**, governed by:

- EHB Global Holding (policy)
- Regional & Country companies (local execution)

CRB issues:

- Professional certifications (doctors, electricians, lawyers, tutors, etc.).
- Product verification certificates (e.g. factory-verified, lab-tested).
- Company verification certificates (e.g. verified business, compliance).

Blockchain Trust Registry (see `blockchain-polkadot.md`, `blockchain-trust-registry.md`) ensures:

- Certificates are **tamper-proof**.
- Status changes (revoked, expired) are visible and auditable.

## 7. GLOBAL LEGAL SYSTEM FLOW

High-level legal structure:

```text
Global Holding Company (EHB Global)
        │
        ▼
Regional Headquarters (Asia / Europe / MENA / Americas / Africa)
        │
        ▼
Country Operating Company (e.g. EHB Technologies Pakistan)
        │
        ▼
Corporate Franchise (Country Level)
        │
        ▼
Master Franchise (Region Level)
        │
        ▼
Sub Franchise (City Level)
```

Each layer has:

- Its own **contracts & legal responsibilities**.
- Clear **escalation & reporting lines**.
- Alignment with **DMO policies and global standards**.

## 8. LEGAL COMPLIANCE AREAS

EHB must comply with multiple legal domains:

- **Business registration laws** (company formation, licenses).
- **Tax regulations** (VAT/GST, corporate tax, reporting).
- **Consumer protection laws** (refunds, dispute rights, transparency).
- **Data protection laws** (GDPR/CCPA/local laws).
- **Payment regulations** (KYC/AML, money services, wallets).

These are reflected in:

- PSS (identity, AML)
- Wallet & payments design
- Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
- Franchise & partner contracts
