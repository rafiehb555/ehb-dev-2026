# EHB GLOBAL FRANCHISE BUSINESS MODEL

> Revenue + Income Distribution System for Sustainable Franchise Network

---

## 1. FRANCHISE LEVELS & ROLES

EHB has **3 franchise levels**:

1. **Sub Franchise (City Level)**
2. **Master Franchise (Regional Level)**
3. **Corporate Franchise (National Level)**

### 1.1 Sub Franchise (City Level)

Operates at:

- City
- Tehsil
- Local area

Responsibilities:

- Business inspection
- Service verification
- Product authenticity checks
- Local complaint investigation

Income sources:

- Inspection fees
- Service verification fees
- Local dispute resolution fees

---

### 1.2 Master Franchise (Regional Level)

Manages:

- ~10–25 Sub Franchises

Responsibilities:

- Audit inspections
- Quality control
- Training sub franchises
- Regional industry coordination

Income sources:

- Percentage from sub-franchise income
- Certification management fees

---

### 1.3 Corporate Franchise (National Level)

Operates at:

- Country level

Responsibilities:

- National verification standards
- Industry board coordination
- Government compliance

Income sources:

- National franchise licensing fees
- Industry verification fees

---

## 2. FRANCHISE REVENUE STREAMS

Franchise holders earn via multiple streams:

### 2.1 Service Verification Fees

Example:

- Plumber registration → **$20** verification fee

Distribution (example split):

| Recipient          | Share |
|--------------------|:-----:|
| Sub Franchise      |  50%  |
| Master Franchise   |  20%  |
| Corporate Franchise|  10%  |
| Platform (EHB HQ)  |  20%  |

---

### 2.2 Product Verification Fees

Example:

- Product authenticity verification → **$50**

Includes:

- Factory inspection
- Quality testing

Franchises earn **per product** verified in their territory.

---

### 2.3 Industry Certification Fees

Example:

- Restaurant certification → **$100**

Includes:

- Food industry verification
- Health inspection
- Quality control certification

Revenue shared across:

- Relevant franchises (Sub/Master/Corporate)
- Platform (for CRB / DMO processing)

---

### 2.4 Refilling (6-Month Renewal)

Every verified service/product must **renew** periodically.

Example:

- Renewal fee → **$30**

Benefits:

- Recurring income for franchises
- Ensures continuous quality control

---

### 2.5 Training Programs

Franchises provide:

- Skill training
- Certification courses
- Industry-standard workshops

Example:

- Electrician certification training program

Revenue:

- Course fees shared between franchise + platform (if online).

---

### 2.6 Dispute Resolution Fees

For complex disputes between:

- Customer and provider
- Provider and franchise

Franchise may charge **mediation / arbitration fees** (regulated by DMO).

---

## 3. MARKETPLACE COMMISSION MODEL

On every marketplace transaction, EHB charges a **commission**.

Example:

- Service booking amount: **$100**

Distribution:

| Recipient          | Share | Amount |
|--------------------|:-----:|-------:|
| Service Provider   |  85%  |  $85   |
| Franchise Network  |  10%  |  $10   |
| Platform (EHB HQ)  |   5%  |   $5   |

Franchise Network share (10%) is further split:

- Sub Franchise (local)  
- Master Franchise (regional)  
- Corporate Franchise (national)

---

## 4. FRANCHISE LICENSE COST STRUCTURE

Illustrative license fees:

- **Sub Franchise** → **$5,000 – $20,000**
- **Master Franchise** → **$50,000 – $200,000**
- **Corporate Franchise** → **$500,000+**

License includes:

- Training
- System access & tools
- Verification authority & branding
- Support + operations playbook

---

## 5. FRANCHISE TERRITORY SYSTEM

Each franchise gets an **exclusive territory**:

- Sub Franchise → City / District
- Master Franchise → Region / Province / State
- Corporate Franchise → Country

Benefits:

- Avoids **territory conflicts**
- Clarifies **revenue rights**
- Encourages investment in local market development

---

## 6. AUTOMATIC INCOME DISTRIBUTION (WALLET-BASED)

All payments flow through **EHB Wallet System**:

1. Customer pays for:
   - Service booking
   - Verification
   - Certification
   - Training
2. Payment enters **platform wallet**.
3. System runs **distribution rules**:

```text
Payment Received
       │
       ▼
 Revenue Split Engine
       │
       ├→ Service Provider Wallet
       ├→ Sub Franchise Wallet
       ├→ Master Franchise Wallet
       ├→ Corporate Franchise Wallet
       └→ Platform Wallet
```

Benefits:

- No manual splits.
- Full transaction history in DMO.
- Easy reporting for all parties.
