## 2️⃣ GLOBAL PROBLEM

**Goal:** Describe the current issues in the digital economy.

Problems to highlight:

- **Fake services**
  - Unverified providers, scams, and low-quality work.
- **Fake or unsafe products**
  - Counterfeit items, health risks, and misleading claims.
- **Unverified professionals**
  - No standard way to verify skills, education, or experience.
- **Trust gaps in marketplaces**
  - Users cannot easily distinguish **who to trust**.

Conclusion:

- The world needs a **trust infrastructure platform** that can:
  - Verify identities, skills, products, and companies.
  - Provide a **standardized, global trust layer** for marketplaces.

---

## 3️⃣ EHB SOLUTION

**Goal:** Present EHB as the answer to the global trust problem.

EHB introduces a **Trust-Based Digital Ecosystem** built on five core systems:

- **DMO – Decentralized Management Office**
  - Central governance and orchestration.
- **JPS – Job Profile & Skill**
  - Verified professional identity and skills.
- **PSS – Proof & Security System**
  - Identity, security, and risk management.
- **CRB – Central Record Blockchain**
  - Certification issuance, registry, and renewal.
- **STL – Service Trust Level**
  - AI-driven trust score and ranking system.

This chapter explains **how these systems work together** to create trust.

  - **Services**
  - **Products**
  - **Professionals**
  - **Companies**
