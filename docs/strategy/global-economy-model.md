# EHB GLOBAL ECONOMY MODEL

*(Platform Revenue, Distribution & Financial Flow)*

This document defines how **EHB generates revenue**, how it **distributes value** across the ecosystem, and how the **financial flow** runs end-to-end. The economy is organized into **6 economic layers**.

---

## 1️⃣ EHB GLOBAL ECONOMY STRUCTURE

EHB’s economy is built on **multiple, complementary revenue sources**:

```text
EHB ECONOMY
│
├ Marketplace Economy
├ Affiliate Economy
├ Franchise Economy
├ Certification Economy
├ Subscription Economy
└ Blockchain Economy
```

This gives the platform **diversified income streams** and makes the ecosystem **sustainable and scalable**.

---

## 2️⃣ MARKETPLACE ECONOMY

The marketplace is EHB’s **largest revenue engine**.

**Sources:**

- Service bookings
- Product sales (GoSellr)
- Professional consultations
- Optional service subscriptions

**Flow:**

```text
Customer books service / buys product
        │
        ▼
Platform service fee / commission
        │
        ▼
Revenue distribution (provider + platform + affiliates + franchise)
```

**Example distribution (service booking):**

| Share             | Percentage |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Service Provider  | 80%       |
| Platform           | 20%       |

From the platform share, value flows to:

- Affiliates (referral / matrix commissions)
- Franchise network (territory / verification share)
- Platform operations and development

Exact splits can be parameterized per product type (services vs products) and region.

---

## 3️⃣ AFFILIATE ECONOMY

The affiliate system acts as EHB’s **global growth and marketing network**.

**Income sources for affiliates:**

- User referrals (signup + activity)
- Business and provider onboarding
- Marketplace transactions from referred users (matrix commissions)
- Franchise sales and referrals

**Flow:**

```text
Affiliate referral
      │
      ▼
New user / business joins
      │
      ▼
User transactions (bookings, sales, certifications)
      │
      ▼
Affiliate commission (direct + matrix levels)
```

Affiliate economy is documented in detail in the **Affiliate Program Model** and **5×10 Matrix** strategy docs.

---

## 4️⃣ FRANCHISE ECONOMY

The franchise network enables **local expansion and on-ground verification**.

**Revenue sources:**

- Franchise license fees (one-time and/or recurring)
- Verification and inspection fees
- Training and certification support programs
- Dispute resolution and mediation fees

**Example distribution (e.g. verification fee):**

| Level                | Share |
|----------------------|------:|
| Sub Franchise        | 40%   |
| Master Franchise     | 30%   |
| Corporate Franchise  | 20%   |
| Platform             | 10%   |

This keeps incentives aligned from city level up to country level.

---

## 5️⃣ CERTIFICATION ECONOMY

Industry and CRB certifications generate **recurring and one-time revenue**.

**Examples:**

- Professional certification fees
- Business verification fees
- Product authenticity and industry certification fees
- Renewal / refilling fees (e.g. 6-month cycle)

**Flow:**

```text
Professional / Business applies for certification
       │
       ▼
Verification + inspection + industry review
       │
       ▼
Certification fee collected
       │
       ▼
Split: CRB / Industry Authority / Franchise / Platform
```

Certification economy ties directly to **trust and quality** and supports the **Global Industry Governance** model.

---

## 6️⃣ SUBSCRIPTION ECONOMY

EHB can offer **premium and subscription-based services**.

**Examples:**

- Premium business profile (featured listing, analytics)
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- Priority service ranking (within policy and STL rules)
- Higher visibility or promotion packs

**Flow:**

```text
Business / Professional upgrades account
       │
       ▼
Monthly / annual subscription fee
       │
       ▼
Platform revenue (with optional affiliate/franchise referral share)
```

Subscriptions add **predictable recurring revenue** alongside transaction-based income.

---

## 7️⃣ BLOCKCHAIN ECONOMY

Blockchain integration adds **on-chain and network-level revenue**.

**Examples:**

- Transaction fees (on-chain certification, verification events)
- Certification registry and verification fees
- Staking rewards for validators and nominators (EHB token)

**Flow:**

```text
Blockchain transaction (e.g. certification anchor)
       │
       ▼
Network / protocol fee
       │
       ▼
Validator and nominator rewards (per tokenomics)
```

Details are in the **Tokenomics & Staking Model** and **Multi-Relay Blockchain** architecture docs.

---

## 8️⃣ REVENUE DISTRIBUTION MODEL (HIGH LEVEL)

Platform revenue is distributed across the ecosystem:

```text
Platform Revenue
│
├ Affiliates (referral + matrix commissions)
├ Franchise Network (verification, inspection, territory share)
├ Validators / Staking (blockchain layer)
├ Platform Operations (infra, support, compliance)
└ Development / Treasury (product, growth, reserves)
```

This keeps the ecosystem **balanced and sustainable** while rewarding growth (affiliates), verification (franchises), and security (blockchain).

---

## 9️⃣ ECONOMIC CYCLE

End-to-end economic flow:

```text
Users (customers, professionals, businesses)
  │
  ▼
Services / Products listed and consumed
  │
  ▼
Marketplace Transactions (bookings, sales, certifications)
  │
  ▼
Platform Fees & Commissions
  │
  ▼
Distribution → Affiliates + Franchise + Platform + Blockchain
```

This cycle **continuously drives platform growth** and aligns incentives across all participants.
