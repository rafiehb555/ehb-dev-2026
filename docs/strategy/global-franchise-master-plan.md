 # EHB GLOBAL FRANCHISE MASTER PLAN

 *(Worldwide Expansion & Verification Network)*

This plan describes how the **EHB franchise network** will expand globally and how it will implement the **verification ecosystem on the ground**, connecting the digital platform to real-world inspections and certifications.

The franchise model is designed in **4 layers**.

---

## 1️⃣ GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS

**Top-level governance and technology center.**

Responsibilities:

- Platform **technology development** and roadmap.
- Defining **global policies** and operating standards.
- Setting **industry standards** and verification frameworks.
- Designing and maintaining the **certification framework** with CRB.
- Managing **global partnerships** (governments, enterprises, regulators).

Global HQ manages:

- **AI systems**
- **Blockchain trust registry**
- Overall **global platform operations** and data governance.

---

## 2️⃣ CORPORATE FRANCHISE (COUNTRY LEVEL)

EHB appoints a **Corporate Franchise** in each country.

Examples:

- EHB Pakistan
- EHB UAE
- EHB UK
- EHB USA

Responsibilities:

- **National franchise management**
  - Appointing and supervising Master Franchises.
- **Industry departments coordination**
  - Country-level coordination with relevant industry bodies.
- **Government and regulatory compliance**
  - Ensuring local laws, licenses, and approvals.
- **National marketing & brand building**
  - Country-level campaigns and partnerships.

Corporate Franchise supervises:

- All **Master Franchises** inside that country.

---

## 3️⃣ MASTER FRANCHISE (REGIONAL LEVEL)

Master Franchises operate at **regional/state/province level**.

Examples:

- Punjab Region
- Sindh Region
- Dubai Region

Responsibilities:

- Managing the **Sub-Franchise network** within the region.
- Conducting **audits of inspections** and verification quality.
- Organizing **training programs** for sub-franchise teams and inspectors.
- Ensuring **verification standards and SOPs** are followed.

Master Franchises act as the **bridge** between Corporate Franchises and local Sub Franchises.

---

## 4️⃣ SUB FRANCHISE (CITY / LOCAL LEVEL)

Sub Franchises are the **local verification authorities**.

Examples:

- Rawalpindi
- Lahore
- Karachi

Responsibilities:

- **Business inspections**
  - Visiting offices, shops, hospitals, factories, etc.
- **Service verification**
  - Confirming that listed services are real, safe, and available.
- **Product authenticity checks**
  - Validating product claims, labeling, and physical stock (when needed).
- **Complaint investigations**
  - Handling local disputes and on-site investigations.

Sub Franchise **inspectors** perform the **field inspections** and submit reports to the system.

---

## FRANCHISE NETWORK STRUCTURE

Overall hierarchy:

```text
EHB Global Headquarters
        │
        ▼
Corporate Franchise (Country)
        │
        ▼
Master Franchise (Region)
        │
        ▼
Sub Franchise (City)
```

This structure ensures:

- Clear **responsibility and escalation paths**.
- Scalable, **decentralized verification capacity**.

---

## FRANCHISE RESPONSIBILITIES (SUMMARY)

Franchise network collectively performs:

- **Business verification**
- **Service inspections**
- **Product authenticity checks**
- **Complaint investigations**
- **Certification and CRB support**

Outcome:

- Strong **on-ground trust** that feeds into:
  - CRB Certification
  - STL Trust Scores
  - Marketplace ranking and visibility.

---

## FRANCHISE INCOME MODEL

Franchises have **multiple revenue sources**:

- **Verification fees**
- **Inspection fees**
- **Certification support services**
- **Training programs and workshops**
- **Dispute resolution and mediation fees**

Example revenue distribution for a **$50 verification fee**:

- **Sub Franchise** → 40%
- **Master Franchise** → 20%
- **Corporate Franchise** → 20%
- **Platform (Global HQ)** → 20%

This creates:

- Clear **financial incentives** at every level to:
  - Maintain quality
  - Grow the network
  - Handle verifications efficiently.

---

## FRANCHISE TERRITORY SYSTEM

Each franchise receives an **exclusive territory** to avoid internal conflicts.

Examples:

- **Sub Franchise** → specific **city / district**.
- **Master Franchise** → specific **region / state / province**.
- **Corporate Franchise** → entire **country**.

Benefits:

- Prevents **overlap and competition** between franchises.
- Allows each franchise to **focus on growth and quality** within its area.

---

## GLOBAL EXPANSION MODEL

Illustrative growth plan:

```text
Year 1 → 1 Country
Year 2 → 5 Countries
Year 3 → 20 Countries
Year 5 → 100+ Countries
```

Expansion strategy:

- Start with a **foundation country**.
- Gradually onboard **Corporate Franchises** in high-priority markets.
- Support them in establishing **Master and Sub Franchises**.

Result:

- Rapid yet structured **global verification coverage**.

## FRANCHISE DASHBOARD

Each franchise level gets an **admin dashboard**.

Key features:

- **Inspection requests management**
- **Verification and certification reports**
- **Certification status tracking**
- **Revenue and commission tracking**
- **Complaint and dispute handling**

Purpose:

- Give franchises **full operational visibility** and control.
- Enable **HQ and Corporate** to monitor performance and compliance.

High-level view:

```text
EHB Verification Network
│
├ Franchise Inspections
```
