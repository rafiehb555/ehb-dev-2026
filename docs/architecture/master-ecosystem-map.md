 # EHB MASTER ECOSYSTEM MAP

 *(Complete Platform Ecosystem Visualization)*

 This document provides a **single high-level ecosystem map** of the entire **EHB Technologies Limited** platform. It is designed for **investors, developers, and strategic partners** to understand the **full system in one view**.

 The ecosystem is organized into **6 major layers**.

 ---

 ## 1️⃣ USER ECOSYSTEM

**Who uses the platform.**

Primary stakeholder groups:

- **Customers** – book services, buy products.
- **Service Providers** – individuals offering local services (plumbers, electricians, doctors, etc.).
- **Professionals** – consultants, experts, knowledge workers.
- **Businesses & Companies** – SMEs, enterprises, chains.
- **Product Sellers** – brands, manufacturers, distributors, and resellers.

On the platform they:

- **Offer services**
- **Sell products**
- **Book verified services**
- **Hire verified professionals**

This layer represents **all demand and supply** entering the ecosystem.

---

 ## 2️⃣ TRUST INFRASTRUCTURE

**EHB’s core innovation – the Trust Layer.**

Core systems:

- **DMO – Decentralized Management Office**
  - Central governance, orchestration, and data control.
- **JPS – Job Profile & Skill**
  - Professional identity and skill profiles for individuals.
- **PSS – Proof & Security System**
  - Identity, device, KYC/KYB, risk and fraud checks.
- **CRB – Certification & Registry Board**
  - Formal certification, registry, and renewal authority.
- **STL – Service Trust Level**
  - AI-driven trust score and ranking engine.

**Purpose:**

- Build **verifiable trust** for every:
  - User
  - Professional
  - Business
  - Service
  - Product

This layer converts raw marketplace activity into a **trustworthy, ranked ecosystem**.

---

 ## 3️⃣ INDUSTRY VERIFICATION NETWORK

**Specialized verification across 32 industries.**

EHB operates a **32-industry verification system** where each industry department acts as an **authority** for its domain.

Industries verify:

- **Companies**
- **Services**
- **Products**
- **Professionals**

Example industries:

- **Health & Medical**
- **Construction & Real Estate**
- **Technology & IT**
- **Education & Training**
- **Energy & Solar**
- **Food & Agriculture**
- **Finance & Banking**
- **Logistics & Delivery**

Key principle:

- A single entity can be **verified by multiple industries** for stronger trust.
  - Example: a **Solar Installation Service** can be verified by:
    - Energy
    - Construction
    - Technology
    - Quality Control

This layer ensures **domain-specific, expert-level verification**.

---

 ## 4️⃣ FRANCHISE INSPECTION NETWORK

**Real-world verification connected to the digital platform.**

Franchise hierarchy:

- **Corporate Franchise** – Country level
- **Master Franchise** – Regional level
- **Sub Franchise** – City / Local level

Core responsibilities:

- **Physical inspections** (sites, offices, shops, factories, hospitals).
- **Business verification** (existence, operations, licenses).
- **Complaint investigation** (on-site checks, interviews, evidence).
- **Support for industry departments and CRB** with ground truth data.

This layer connects:

> **Digital trust systems (DMO, PSS, CRB, STL)** ↔ **Physical world inspections**

---

 ## 5️⃣ DIGITAL PLATFORM

**The Super App layer that users directly interact with.**

Main components:

- **AI Marketplace**
  - Global discovery of services, professionals, companies, and products.
  - AI-powered ranking based on STL, verification, reviews, and relevance.
- **Service Booking System**
  - End-to-end booking, scheduling, and completion flows.
- **Product Marketplace (GoSellr)**
  - Verified product listings with multi-industry verification.
- **Professional Profiles**
  - JPS-based profiles with skills, experience, and certifications.
- **Wallet & Payments**
  - Wallet balances, payouts, commissions, and multi-currency support.
- **Complaint & Dispute System**
  - Customer protection, resolution workflows, and STL impact.

This layer turns the trust infrastructure into **real user-facing value**:

- Easier discovery
- Safer transactions
- Higher quality providers

---

 ## 6️⃣ TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE

**The technical backbone that runs the ecosystem.**

Core technologies:

- **AI Engine**
  - Trust scoring (STL), ranking, recommendations, fraud detection, insights.
- **Microservices Architecture**
  - Independent services for Users, PSS, JPS, Marketplace, Franchise, Industries, CRB, STL, Wallet, etc.
- **Cloud Infrastructure**
  - Global, multi-region deployment with load balancing and auto-scaling.
- **Blockchain Trust Registry**
  - Tamper-proof registry for key certifications, verifications, and trust events.
- **Global Databases & Search**
  - PostgreSQL, Redis, Elasticsearch, MongoDB (as defined in data architecture docs).

This layer ensures:

- **Scalability** to millions of users.
- **Resilience** and uptime.
- **Compliance** with data sovereignty and security standards.
