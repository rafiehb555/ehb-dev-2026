# EHB GLOBAL INDUSTRY GOVERNANCE SYSTEM

*(32 Industries – Multi-Level Authority & Quality Control)*

This document defines the **global industry governance structure** for EHB. It ensures that **all 32 industries** are managed professionally and that **quality and trust** are maintained across the platform through a clear hierarchy from global policy to local inspection.

The system is organized into **5 governance layers**.

---

## 1️⃣ GLOBAL INDUSTRY GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

Each industry has a **multi-level authority structure**:

```text
EHB INDUSTRY GOVERNANCE
│
├ Global Industry Council
├ Regional Industry Boards
├ National Industry Authorities
├ Franchise Inspection Network
└ Certified Professionals
```

This creates a **global → local verification and governance network**.

---

## 2️⃣ GLOBAL INDUSTRY COUNCIL

**Top-level authority** for each industry domain.

Responsibilities:

- Define **industry standards** (safety, quality, ethics).
- Set **certification rules** and renewal policies.
- Maintain **global compliance** and interoperability.
- Issue **policy updates** and best-practice guidelines.

Examples:

- Health Global Council
- Technology Global Council
- Education Global Council
- Construction Global Council
- … (one per industry or per industry cluster)

Members:

- Senior industry experts
- Certified professionals with proven track record
- External industry advisors and regulators (where applicable)

---

## 3️⃣ REGIONAL INDUSTRY BOARDS

Regional boards manage **multiple countries** within a geography.

Example regions:

- Middle East
- South Asia
- Europe
- North America
- Africa
- Oceania

Responsibilities:

- Implement **regional policies** aligned with Global Council.
- **Approve certifications** and escalate edge cases.
- **Resolve cross-country industry disputes**.
- Coordinate with National Authorities and Franchises.

---

## 4️⃣ NATIONAL INDUSTRY AUTHORITIES

Each country can have **national industry authorities** per domain.

Examples:

- EHB Health Authority Pakistan
- EHB Technology Authority UAE
- EHB Education Authority UK

Responsibilities:

- **Local legal and regulatory compliance**.
- **Industry registrations** and professional verification.
- Liaison with **local franchises** and inspection network.
- Reporting to Regional Boards and Global Council.

---

## 5️⃣ FRANCHISE INSPECTION NETWORK

Franchises perform **ground-level verification and inspection**.

Structure:

```text
Corporate Franchise (Country)
      │
      ▼
Master Franchise (Region)
      │
      ▼
Sub Franchise (City / Local)
```

Responsibilities:

- **Service and business inspections**.
- **Complaint investigation** and evidence collection.
- **Local verification** and report submission to Industry Authorities.
- Support for **refilling and re-certification** cycles.

---

## 6️⃣ CERTIFIED PROFESSIONALS (BASE LAYER)

The base of the governance pyramid is **verified professionals**.

Examples:

- Doctors, nurses, medical staff
- Engineers, architects, contractors
- Teachers, trainers, education providers
- Lawyers, legal advisors
- Developers, IT professionals
- … (across all 32 industries)

Requirements:

- **Identity verification** (PSS).
- **Industry certification** (CRB + relevant industry authority).
- **STL trust score** within acceptable range.
- Ongoing **refilling** and compliance with industry standards.

---

## 7️⃣ CERTIFICATION FLOW

End-to-end professional certification process:

```text
User applies for certification
      │
      ▼
JPS Profile created / updated
      │
      ▼
Skill & document verification
      │
      ▼
Franchise inspection (where required)
      │
      ▼
Industry Authority review
      │
      ▼
Certification issued (CRB + Industry)
      │
      ▼
Record stored (DB + optional blockchain)
```

Certification can be **anchored on blockchain** for tamper-proof proof.

---

## 8️⃣ INDUSTRY COMPLAINT SYSTEM

When a customer or partner raises a complaint:

```text
Customer / Partner Complaint
       │
       ▼
Franchise investigation (evidence, site visit)
       │
       ▼
Industry Board / Authority review
       │
       ▼
Decision (warning / penalty / suspension / revoke)
       │
       ▼
STL update & service visibility adjustment
```

Possible outcomes:

- **Warning** (no STL change or small penalty).
- **STL penalty** (score reduction).
- **Service temporarily hidden** or suspended.
- **Certification revoked** in severe cases.

---

## 9️⃣ INDUSTRY PERFORMANCE MONITORING

AI and analytics continuously track:

- **Service success rate** per provider and per industry.
- **Customer satisfaction** and review trends.
- **Complaint ratio** and resolution time.
- **STL score distribution** and anomalies.

Example automated response:

```text
High complaints or low STL cluster detected
      │
      ▼
Industry alert generated
      │
      ▼
Targeted inspection or audit triggered
```

This keeps **quality and trust** under constant oversight.

---

High-level map:

```text
EHB GLOBAL INDUSTRY GOVERNANCE
│
├ Global Industry Councils (standards, policy)
├ Regional Industry Boards (multi-country coordination)
```

Together with:
