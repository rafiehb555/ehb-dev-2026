 # EHB 32 INDUSTRIES COMPLETE STRUCTURE

 *(Industry → Sub-Sector → Service/Product Model)*

This document defines the **complete structural model** for the **32 Industries** in the EHB ecosystem. Each industry is organized into:

- **Industry Level** – High-level domain.
- **Sub-Sector Level** – Specific verticals within the industry.
- **Service / Product Level** – Types of services and products typically verified.

This structure is the **verification backbone** for the platform.

## MULTI-INDUSTRY VERIFICATION MODEL

EHB’s powerful concept:

> **A single service or company can be verified by multiple industries.**

Example – **Solar installation company:**

- Verified by:
  - **Energy Industry**
  - **Construction & Engineering Industry**
  - **Technology & IT Industry**
  - **Quality Control & Testing Industry**

Result:

- **STL trust score increases** significantly through multi-industry verification.
- Trust card shows multiple industry badges, boosting user confidence.

## INDUSTRY STRUCTURE MODEL

Generic structure for each industry:

```text
Industry
   │
   ├ Sub-Sectors
   │
   ├ Companies
   │
   ├ Services
   │
   └ Products
```

Each industry maintains its own:

- **Certification board**
- **Compliance standards**
- **Inspection and audit system**
