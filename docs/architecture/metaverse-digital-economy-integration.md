 # EHB METAVERSE & DIGITAL ECONOMY INTEGRATION

 *(Future Digital Services Ecosystem)*

This document outlines how EHB can extend into **virtual environments and digital economies**, integrating metaverse-style experiences with its trust and certification infrastructure.

---

## 1️⃣ METAVERSE USE CASES

Potential features:

- **Virtual business offices**
  - Verified companies host virtual spaces for client meetings.
- **Virtual training centers**
  - Training institutes and experts run immersive classes and workshops.
- **Virtual service consultations**
  - Professionals (doctors, lawyers, consultants) provide avatar-based sessions.
- **Virtual certification exams**
  - Proctored exams and assessments inside virtual environments.

Example:

```text
User joins virtual law consultation
        │
        ▼
Verified lawyer avatar interaction
        │
        ▼
Session data & outcomes linked to EHB trust systems
```

---

## 2️⃣ DIGITAL ECONOMY SYSTEM

EHB can create a **digital asset ecosystem** layered on top of its trust infrastructure.

Possible assets:

- **Certification NFTs**
  - On-chain, non-transferable credentials for professionals and businesses.
- **Digital service licenses**
  - Time-bound or scope-bound licenses represented as tokens.
- **Virtual professional profiles**
  - Rich identity representations usable across virtual worlds.

Example:

```text
Doctor Certificate
      │
      ▼
On-chain NFT Credential
      │
      ▼
Usable across EHB apps + partner metaverse platforms
```
