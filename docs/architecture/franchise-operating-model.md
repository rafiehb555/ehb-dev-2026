- Physical verifications (CRB inspections)
- Local service provider onboarding
- Customer support
- Quality control

# FRANCHISE REVENUE MODEL

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Source | Distribution |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Transaction Commission | 2-5% per transaction | HQ 40% / Corp 25% / Master 20% / Sub 15% |
| Verification Fees | Per PSS verification | HQ 50% / Sub 50% |
| Certification Fees | Per CRB certification | HQ 30% / Master 35% / Sub 35% |
| Subscription Fees | STL premium plans | HQ 60% / Corp 20% / Master 10% / Sub 10% |
| Franchise License | Annual license fee | HQ 100% |

## Revenue Distribution Example

For a ₨10,000 transaction with 3% commission (₨300):

| Recipient | Percentage | Amount |
|-----------|:----------:|-------:|
| EHB HQ | 40% | ₨120 |
| Corporate Franchise | 25% | ₨75 |
| Master Franchise | 20% | ₨60 |
| Sub Franchise | 15% | ₨45 |
