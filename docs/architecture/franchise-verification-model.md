# EHB FRANCHISE VERIFICATION MODEL

> How Corporate / Master / Sub Franchises power physical & multi-industry verification

---

## 1. FRANCHISE STRUCTURE (VERIFICATION VIEW)

EHB franchise system is the **physical trust network** that connects the digital platform with real-world verification.

Three levels:

1. **Sub Franchise (Local Level)** – City / Tehsil
2. **Master Franchise (Regional Level)** – District / Region
3. **Corporate Franchise (National Level)** – Country

Conceptual tree:

```text
EHB Global HQ (EHB Technologies Limited)
           │
           ▼
   Corporate Franchise (Country)
           │
           ▼
    Master Franchises (Regions)
           │
           ▼
    Sub Franchises (Cities)
           │
           ▼
  Local Service Providers / Companies
```

## 2. FRANCHISE RESPONSIBILITIES (VERIFICATION-FOCUSED)

### 2.1 Sub Franchise (Local Level)

Operates at **city / tehsil** level.

Responsibilities:

- Local **business verification**
- Service provider **physical inspection**
- Product **authenticity verification**
- Local **complaint investigation**
- **Document collection** for PSS (identity, licenses, permits)
- **Skill test arrangement** for CRB
- **Industry inspection coordination** (with relevant authorities)

Example:

- Doctor, plumber, factory, restaurant, salon, electrician → **Sub Franchise** visits, inspects, and reports.

---

### 2.2 Master Franchise (Regional Level)

Operates at **district / region** level.

Responsibilities:

- Manage **10–25 Sub Franchises**
- Verify **inspection reports** from sub level
- Coordinate **industry verifications** (multiple industries)
- Conduct **quality audits**
- Monitor **STL anomalies** in their region
- Train sub franchises on updated standards
- Lead **fraud investigations** on regional cases

Example:

- If a sub franchise did false verification, **Master Franchise** audits and escalates to CRB/DMO as needed.

---

### 2.3 Corporate Franchise (National Level)

Operates at **country** level.

Responsibilities:

- Ensure **national regulation compliance**
- Coordinate with **industry boards & authorities**
- Oversee **CRB certification** standards implementation
- Investigate **major complaints** (hospitals, large factories, chains)
- Define **national verification standards**
- Maintain **government partnerships**

Example:

- Hospital chains, national manufacturing groups, or large universities → Corporate Franchise supervises final approval.

## 3. FRANCHISE ROLE IN EHB ECOSYSTEM

Franchise network supports and feeds into **5 core systems**:

- `DMO` – decision, registry, workflows
- `JPS` – professional profiles and skills
- `PSS` – identity and risk checks
- `CRB` – certification and registry
- `STL` – trust scoring and visibility

Franchise activities:

- Physical inspections
- Document and evidence gathering
- Local **industry testing & validation**
- On-ground **compliance monitoring**
- Dispute and complaint handling

Result:

- Digital trust (STL) always backed by **real-world verification**.

## 7. 6-MONTH REFILLING SYSTEM (FRANCHISE EXECUTION)

Refilling ensures trust remains **fresh and valid**.

Cycle:

1. Inspection
2. Testing
3. Certification renewal

Cadence (example):

- Many industries: **every 6 months**
- Some (e.g. manufacturing): **every 12 months**

Enforcement:

- If provider fails to complete refilling within grace period (e.g. 1 week):
  - Listings can be **hidden** or flagged.
  - STL score is **reduced**.

All field re-inspections are coordinated via:

- **Sub Franchises** (local)
- **Master Franchises** (regional oversight)
- **Corporate Franchise + DMO** (national control)
