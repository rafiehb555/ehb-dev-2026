 # EHB FINAL MASTER SYSTEM MAP

 *(Complete Global Ecosystem Structure – Platform Blueprint)*

This document defines the **EHB Final Master System Map** – a complete mega structure of the entire **EHB ecosystem**, showing how:

- Users
- Industries
- Franchises
- AI systems
- Blockchain
- Marketplace
- Databases

all connect into a single, coherent architecture.

The system is organized into **8 major layers**.

---

## 1️⃣ GLOBAL USER ECOSYSTEM

**Primary participants on the platform.**

User groups:

- **Customers**
- **Professionals**
- **Businesses & Companies**
- **Product Sellers**
- **Franchise Owners**
- **Industry Authorities**

These users interact with:

- Marketplace
- Verification processes
- Certifications
- Wallet & payments

This layer represents **all actors** who generate activity, data, and value in the system.

---

## 2️⃣ DIGITAL PLATFORM LAYER

**Where users directly use the EHB platform.**

Main systems:

- **AI Marketplace**
  - Unified discovery for services, professionals, companies, and products.
- **Service Booking System**
  - Search → selection → booking → completion → review.
- **Product Marketplace**
  - Verified products listing, cart, and orders.
- **Professional Network**
  - JPS-based profiles, endorsements, and discovery.
- **Wallet & Payment System**
  - Payments, payouts, commissions, and revenue sharing.
- **Complaint & Dispute System**
  - Customer protection, mediation, and resolution.

This layer makes EHB a **Super App ecosystem**, combining multiple use cases into a single experience.

---

## 3️⃣ TRUST INFRASTRUCTURE

**Core innovation layer** responsible for trust and verification.

Components:

- **DMO – Decentralized Management Office**
  - Governance, policy, and data orchestration.
- **JPS – Job Profile & Skill**
  - Verified professional identity and skill system.
- **PSS – Proof & Security System**
  - Identity, device, KYC/KYB, risk and fraud checks.
- **CRB – Central Record Blockchain**
  - Certification issuance, registry, refilling, and renewal.
- **STL – Service Trust Level**
  - AI-driven trust scoring and ranking engine.

Purpose:

- Maintain **real identities and verified entities**.
- Provide **trust scores and certifications** that power the marketplace.

---

## 4️⃣ INDUSTRY VERIFICATION NETWORK

**Specialized verification layer with 32 industries.**

Each industry is responsible for **domain-specific verification** of:

- **Services**
- **Products**
- **Professionals**
- **Companies**

Example industries:

- Health & Medical
- Technology & IT
- Construction & Engineering
- Energy
- Education & Training
- Food & Restaurant
- Finance
- Logistics & Transportation
- Textile & Fashion
- Tourism & Hospitality
… and others up to **32**.

Key principle:

- A single entity (e.g., a business or service) can be **verified by multiple industries**, creating deep multi-angle trust.

---

## 5️⃣ FRANCHISE INSPECTION NETWORK

**Physical verification layer** connecting digital systems to the real world.

Structure:

- **Corporate Franchise → Country**
- **Master Franchise → Region**
- **Sub Franchise → City / Local**

Responsibilities:

- **Business inspections**
- **Service verification**
- **Product authenticity checks**
- **Complaint investigations**

Franchise inspectors perform **on-ground inspections** and feed data into:

- Industry departments
- CRB
- STL trust scoring

This layer ensures trust is based on **both digital data and real-world checks**.

---

**Intelligence layer** that makes the platform smart and self-optimizing.

Modules:

- **Search Ranking AI**
- **Recommendation Engine**
- **Fraud Detection AI**
- **Complaint Analysis AI**
- **Trust Score Optimization**

Responsibilities:

- Continuously analyze:
  - Behavior

  - Reviews and complaints
  - Verification and certification data
- Optimize:
  - Search results and recommendations
  - STL scores and risk levels
  - Fraud detection and anomaly alerts

AI acts across **Digital Platform, Trust Infrastructure, and Data Systems**.

---

## 7️⃣ DATA & DATABASE SYSTEM

**Large-scale data storage and management layer.**

Main databases:

- **User Database**
- **Business Database**
- **Service Database**
- **Product Database**
- **Inspection Database**
- **Certification Database**
- **Review & Complaint Database**
- **Trust Score (STL) Database**
- **Wallet & Transactions Database**
- **AI Data & Logs**

This layer:

- Supports operational queries, analytics, and AI training.
- Integrates with **search engines** and **caches** for performance.

---

## 8️⃣ BLOCKCHAIN TRUST REGISTRY

**Tamper-proof trust and certification registry.**

Stored data:

- **Certification records**
- **Verification event logs**
- **Trust history snapshots**
- **Certificate hashes**

Benefits:

- Global, independent **verification of certificates and trust records**.
- Strong protection against tampering and retrospective manipulation.

This layer complements internal databases by providing **external verifiability**.
